# Log 28-09-2026 Brainstorming and project startup

## Summary

The theme for Prototype 5 was introduced: **“The baby is playing in the room.”**

The theme did not align with the planned final project. The group therefore decided to adapt Prototype 5 to support the final game.

## Discussion and Decisions

### Gameplay Loop

The gameplay loop needed to be simple and straightforward. Three ideas were considered:

1. Watch videos and compare them with how the baby is playing.
2. Observe how the baby is playing and choose the correct answers.
3. Listen to the sounds the baby makes and use them to determine which toy to give the baby.

The group prioritized the third option, which focuses on audio.

This approach makes better use of the group’s skills: there are more audio specialists than visual artists, and most members have stronger coding skills.

### Scope and Reuse

Prototype 5 will focus on creating assets and game systems that can be reused in the final project, rather than becoming a standalone game.

This decision aims to make the best use of the group’s time and effort.

### Technology

The group decided to use **Godot with GDScript** instead of C#, with web export as a key consideration.

GDScript’s similarity to Python was also considered an advantage, making it easier to learn.
