# Log 29-09-2026 Game Design Meeting / Creating the design pillars

## Summary

The main goal was to create a better understanding on, how the game needs to function. During the day, three design
pillars were made. These pillars should be the ground foundation of the whole game.

## Discussion and Decisions
The group had big concerns about the current state of the planning. One of the group members could not see the full picture of the game.
They wanted to narrow down exactly, what the game is trying to be, and how the gameplay loop would look like. The game designers and producer were
talking about EXPERIENCE & EMOTIONS, GAMEPLAY LOOP, and DESIGN PILLARS. After a big discussion about player experience and gameplay loop, the possibility
of creating the design pillars was possible. This made the vision much more clear, about what the game should be.

### Design Pillars
The most important part of the meeting, was creating the design pillars. Each pillar was set to focus on a specific area of the game. 
1. BIOLOGICAL HORROR - Body horror, The unknown, Disease, Dead bodies
2. SCIENTIST - Give a sense of ownership to the "research/discovery". Knowledge Gates hidden in plain sight. Eureka moments
3. SAFE THE COMMUNITY - Doing "horrible" things for the colony, The colony is isolated

The first pillar focuses on the horror aspect of the game. The pillar is the foundation of how the horror should be implemented. The horror elements
will have a strong connection to how the virus affect the dead bodies, and also scary imagery and information on the computer.

The second pillar focuses on the gameplay and player experience. It is important that, the player feels like they are discovering something, using
the scientific method. First they need to make some research about the virus, then make a hypothesis, test the hypothesis and finally document
the results. Creating this feeling is essential for this pillar.

The las pillar focuses on the story and mood of the game. The story needs to give the player a feeling of contribution to the colony/community
by doing some experiments. They need to feel responsible for saving the colony. We ended up deciding that the player needs to feel bad about what
they are doing in the lab, but they should know that it is necessary to save the colony.
